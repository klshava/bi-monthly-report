BI Team Member Spotlight

Kudzai "The Ledger" Shava

Origin

Legend has it Kudzai was forged, not born: assembled sometime in Q2 from spare Asana GIDs and unresolved helpdesk threads. Nobody has seen the moment of creation. There are theories.

BI Superpower

Sheer throughput, touching Compliance/RSG, Marketing and Operations across AU, US and UK. The Sustainable Wagering summary, the Compliance EOM dashboard and extract, the Social Dabblers report, Premium Prospecting, World Cup Bet Club, retention rate work, copy-bet stats: if it touched three regions and needed doing fast, it probably has Kudzai's name on it.

Origin Story, Corrected

Actually just a very committed BI analyst from Harare with a genuine gift for making compliance data behave. The PayID name-matching fix from June still pays dividends: the false positive rate stayed down all July. Drop Kudzai a line if your dashboard has ever silently broken and mysteriously started working again, there's a decent chance that was them.

Interested In

Turning "please just fix the false positives" into an actual root-cause fix instead of a patch. Ask about the SMSF/corporate trustee account handling if you want to see the enthusiasm in person.
