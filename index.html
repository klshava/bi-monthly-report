Kudzai: Global Betting Errors Dashboard

AU, UK & US

Featured
🇦🇺 AU 🇬🇧 UK 🇺🇸 US

Built a new cross-region error monitoring dashboard, bringing together bet rejection data from Grafana/Victoria Metrics with actual bet placement volumes from source Postgres, giving the team a single reliable view of error rates across AU, UK and US for the first time. Along the way, reconciled a long-standing discrepancy between reported "bets placed" figures and warehouse data, tracing the gap back to specific rejection categories (fixture closed, market closed) that were being folded into the headline placement numbers. The dashboard breaks down error rates by region, by day (to track whether recent fixes are working), and by copied vs original bets, surfacing that copied bets currently fail at a notably higher rate than original ones. Also added a bet-type breakdown, which uncovered a data quality gap worth flagging: the majority of error events aren't currently tagged with a bet type at all, limiting how far that dimension can be sliced today.
